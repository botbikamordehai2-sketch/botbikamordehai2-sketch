### 🎯 What NEXUS delivers

A research engagement produces four deliverables:

- **Executive Report** — the key findings on one page
- **Competitive Intelligence** — how you compare with competitors, channel by channel
- **Market Analysis** — the segment, the players, and the data behind it
- **Recommendations** — concrete next steps

Turnaround: **7 days**.

### 🧭 How I work

- **Human-in-the-loop.** No outreach, publishing or external write happens without explicit approval.
- **Evidence labels.** Every claim is tagged FACT / OBSERVATION / HYPOTHESIS / MISSING_DATA, so you know what is verified and what isn't.
- **Public sources first.** Research is built from official pages and publicly available data, and coverage gaps are reported rather than filled in with guesses.
- **Agents + automation.** Multi-agent workflows (GitHub, Notion, monday.com, Slack, n8n) handle collection and structuring. I handle judgment and QA.
